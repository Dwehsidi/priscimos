Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains how Priscimos Enterprise Solutions LLC, doing business as PEG Solutions ("we", "us"), handles information you share through priscimosenterprise.net.

What we collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we collect what you choose to send: your name, company or facility, phone number, email address, pickup and drop-off locations, dates and delivery details.

Please don't send health information

Our website form is not meant for protected health information. Please don't include patient names, medical record numbers or test details. We handle that information only as part of a scheduled delivery, under our HIPAA procedures.

How we use it

We use your information to answer your request, prepare quotes, schedule and complete deliveries, and keep ordinary business records.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, such as email hosting, or when the law requires it.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
